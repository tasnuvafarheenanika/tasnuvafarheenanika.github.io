Hi, I am Tasnuva Farheen, an Assistant Professor in the Division of Computer Science at Louisiana State University. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Florida, FICS and FINS labs, in the summer of 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Domenic Forte. My research establishes the foundation for trustworthy hybrid quantum-classical computing and quantum communication, bridging the fields of hardware security, quantum systems, and quantum information. New technology always intrigues me, and I am currently passionate about quantum. I am particularly interested in how hardware evolves over time as technology advances and how it fails in terms of security and resilience. My team focuses on uncovering vulnerabilities and building secure architectures for both NISQ and Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing Systems. We are also actively doing research on building quantum information-guided secure and reliable quantum communication network.   

## Research Interests

- Hardware security
- Chiplet and heterogeneous integration security
- Quantum security: Trustworthy Hybrid Quantum-Classical Computing System
- Quantum Communication and Information
